## EMI-to-income ratio

The EMI-to-income ratio is total monthly loan repayments divided by monthly take-home income. Lenders use similar measures to judge how much more a borrower can take on. A common rule of thumb is to keep total EMIs below roughly 30 to 40 percent of take-home pay.

## Debt and financial resilience

Loan EMIs keep running when income falls, so high fixed repayments shorten the cash runway during an income shock. Paying down high-interest debt reduces this fixed burden and often beats the after-tax return available from low-risk investments.
