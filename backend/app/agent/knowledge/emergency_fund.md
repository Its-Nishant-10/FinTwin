## Emergency fund

An emergency fund is money kept in safe, easily accessible places, such as a savings account, sweep deposit or liquid fund, to cover essential expenses if income stops. A common rule of thumb is three to six months of essential expenses, more for people with irregular income or dependants.

## Cash runway

Cash runway is how many months available cash can cover expenses and loan EMIs after income falls. It is cash divided by the monthly gap between outgoings and reduced income. When the runway is shorter than the income shock, the household must cut spending, borrow or sell investments, often at a bad time.

## Why the emergency fund protects investments

Without an emergency fund, an income shock often forces investors to stop their SIP or sell investments during a market fall. Keeping emergency money separate means long-term investments can be left alone when they are down.
